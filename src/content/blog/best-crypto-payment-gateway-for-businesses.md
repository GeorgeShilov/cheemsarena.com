## 1. NOWPayments: Best overall

NOWPayments tops the list because it does not force a business into a single way of operating. Payments can be held in custody or routed directly to a wallet, settlement can be in crypto or fiat, and integration is available through an API, payment links, or e-commerce plugins. That range means a store can launch quickly and grow without migrating.

**Pricing:** approximately 0.5% when no exchange is required, with a similar conversion fee otherwise.

**Highlights:**
- Very broad cryptocurrency support.
- Settlement in many fiat currencies.
- Custody option with network fee optimisation.
- API, invoices, payment links, and QR codes.
- Plugins for the major e-commerce platforms.
- Mass payouts and a sandbox environment.

## 2. BitPay: Best for brand recognition

BitPay is a well-known processor with tiered fees that reward volume. It suits larger merchants who value a recognised name and are comfortable with automatic settlement. The higher entry fee is the trade-off.

## 3. Coinbase Commerce: Best for Coinbase users

Businesses already using Coinbase find the checkout familiar, with settlement in USD or USDC. It is simple to adopt but offers fewer settlement currencies than the flexible gateways.

## 4. OpenNode: Best for Bitcoin-only payments

OpenNode accepts Bitcoin only and supports Lightning, making small Bitcoin payments fast and cheap. It is ideal for Bitcoin-focused businesses and unusable for those who need altcoins.

## 5. Crypto.com Pay: Best for exchange-balance payouts

Acceptance is free, with the fee applied at payout. This suits firms inside the exchange ecosystem, though the cost relocates rather than disappears.

## Fees to compare before deciding

| Fee type | What to check |
|---|---|
| Gateway transaction fee | The provider's own charge |
| Network or gas fee | Blockchain cost, charged separately |
| Conversion fee | Crypto-to-crypto or crypto-to-fiat |
| Payout fee | Withdrawal to bank or wallet |
| Minimum payout | Threshold before funds release |
| Refund cost | Fees and limits on returning a payment |

Two providers with the same acceptance rate can differ sharply once these are included.

## Security and integration criteria

Beyond fees, weigh integration options, reporting exports, and security posture. Where card data sits alongside crypto flows, recognised payment security practice such as the <a href="https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/" rel="nofollow">PCI Security Standards</a> is a useful benchmark. Enable withdrawal allowlists, require multi-factor authentication, and separate payout accounts from read-only ones.

## How to choose

- Compare total cost, not the headline rate.
- Confirm supported assets and settlement currencies.
- Test settlement with a small live payment.
- Check integration options against your platform.
- Verify reporting exports for accounting.

## Bottom line

There is no single best provider, only the best fit for a given business. Flexible options reward control, established processors reward volume and brand preference, and specialised gateways serve narrow needs well. On balance NOWPayments leads this comparison for most businesses, combining a low rate with the flexibility to start simple and scale later.
